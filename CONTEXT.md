# Vaishnav Mhetre Portfolio

The personal portfolio + Markdown blog authored by Vaishnav Mhetre, hosted free on GitHub Pages, generated by Hugo. Purpose: public career exposure — curating a career that spans motor-insurance platform engineering (Allianz) and market-data pipeline engineering (NielsenIQ/GfK), plus ongoing engineering writing.

## Language

**Portfolio**: the public website itself — the curated frame around the work.
_Avoid_: site, homepage (when referring to the whole thing)

**Case study**: a writeup of one piece of professional work: problem → alternatives & tradeoffs → decision → anonymized outcome. No proprietary code, data, or client specifics.
_Avoid_: project page (too vague), showcase

**Blog post**: a Markdown-authored article targeting an engineer-reader's search intent. Lives under `content/posts/`.
_Avoid_: article (when specific to this site), "content"

**Document engineering**: the discipline of handling insurance documents at scale — data handling, storage, streaming, encoding, resilience, and cost-driven archival.
_Avoid_: file processing (too generic)

**Market data**: retail measurement data processed by the NielsenIQ/GfK ecosystem — POS pipelines, pricing insights, analytics export.
_Avoid_: data analytics (ambiguous, overused)

**Insurance domain**: motor-insurance subject-matter expertise across Contract, Claims, and Document lifecycles from Allianz.
_Avoid_: insurance (when the specific domain expertise matters)

**The frame**: the portfolio's role — a curated narrative hub that points at LinkedIn (where recruiters find you) and GitHub (where code lives). The portfolio is the frame, not the painting.
_Avoid_: (no synonym; keep the metaphor)

**Exposure**: the goal — public visibility to engineers (primary) and recruiters (secondary).
_Avoid_: reach, visibility (when the strategic goal is meant)

**IP-safe**: the rule that corporate work appears only as approach, design, and code-quality narrative — never literal solutions or proprietary detail.
_Avoid_: NDA-safe (covers a subset; the rule is broader)
