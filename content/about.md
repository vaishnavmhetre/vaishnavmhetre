I am a platform engineer with approximately seven years of experience working primarily in **Java**, **TypeScript**, and **Node.js** on **GCP** with **Spring Boot**.

My career spans two domains that shaped how I think about systems at scale:

- **NielsenIQ** — I work on **binary data delivery at scale**: querying retail measurement data from databases, moving it through a delivery pipeline's filters and enrichments, and shipping it to downstream systems as binary payloads rather than documents. I have contributed to the pipeline's configuration-handling framework, its file-handling service, and supervised the migration of prediction services (such as a pricing forecast backend) onto the delivery pipeline — making them robust. I now work on AI-driven initiatives.
- **Allianz Technology** — I worked on a motor-insurance platform, operating at the intersection of Contract, Claims, and Document lifecycles. I led a five-person document team and drove the shift toward event-driven systems and microservices.

Across both roles, the common thread is **data delivery** — taking data out of databases and getting it, reliably and at scale, into the form downstream consumers actually need: binary payloads today, documents earlier in my career.

This portfolio is **the frame** — a curated narrative hub that points at [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vaishnav-mhetre/) (where recruiters find me) and [GitHub](https://github.com/vaishnavmhetre) (where code lives). The frame, not the painting.
