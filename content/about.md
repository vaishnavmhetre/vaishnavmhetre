I am a platform engineer with approximately six years of experience working primarily in **Java**, **TypeScript**, and **Node.js** on **GCP** with **Spring Boot**.

My career spans two domains that shaped how I think about systems at scale:

- **NielsenIQ** — I built and maintained market-data pipelines, processing retail measurement data (POS transactions, pricing insights, analytics export) at throughput that demanded event-driven architecture and careful cost management.
- **Allianz Technology** — I worked on a motor-insurance platform, operating at the intersection of Contract, Claims, and Document lifecycles. I led a five-person document team and drove the shift toward event-driven systems and microservices.

Across both roles, the common thread has been **document engineering** — the discipline of handling insurance documents at scale: data handling, storage, streaming, encoding, resilience, and cost-driven archival.

This portfolio is **the frame** — a curated narrative hub that points at [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vaishnav-mhetre/) (where recruiters find me) and [GitHub](https://github.com/vaishnavmhetre) (where code lives). The frame, not the painting.
